Questions?

The short answers.

Do I need a reservation?

No for a same-day walk-in: take a free seat and enjoy something from the café. For future days, send a request by the day before; we confirm personally.

What is available?

Wi-Fi, power, self-service coffee, portable monitors, books and creative materials. We look after the shared room so everyone can work comfortably.

What is coming next?

AI tools, headphones, more games and activities are being explored. Meeting booths depend on the Club gaining enough members.

How many people can come?

The room holds up to fourteen guests, with eleven reservable places and three walk-in-only places. See the live opening days and hours in the visit planner above.
