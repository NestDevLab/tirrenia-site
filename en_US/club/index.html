Questions?

The short answers.

Do I need a reservation?

No for a same-day walk-in: take a free seat and enjoy something from the café. For a future day, choose your places and confirm online.

What is available?

Wi-Fi, power, self-service coffee, portable monitors, books and creative materials. We look after the shared room so everyone can work comfortably.

What is coming next?

AI tools, headphones, more games and activities are being explored. Meeting booths depend on the Club gaining enough members.

When can I book or cancel?

Book from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead. Bookings and free cancellation close at 18:00 the day before. Check in at the counter from 30 minutes before your start.

Can I use a carnet when I walk in?

Yes. Ask us at the counter to use a carnet visit. Online bookings are confirmed immediately when covered by a carnet visit or a paid single visit.

How many people can come?

Online bookings are for parties of up to ten. The planner shows current opening times and free places. You can make up to three future bookings.
