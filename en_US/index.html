The ritual at the counter

Fire, earth,
coffee.

Ristretto pulled on the lever. Sfogliatella that shatters. Babà soaked in rum and citrus. Saltimbocca alla romana. Parmigiana di melanzane. Warm dishes made with time and care.

Come to the counter for a real Italian ristretto, at an honest price.

From the counter, the route opens to the coast.

The journey north

To the sea.
To the Mediterranean.

Sun, salt, wind and blue horizons. We carry the best of Southern Italy to our shelves and to your table — a small gastronomic route from the coast to the counter.

Pasta, olive oil, preserves, tomatoes, tuna, capers, lemon biscuits and more.

Up north, towards Haarlem.
