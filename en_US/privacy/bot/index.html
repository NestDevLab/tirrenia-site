Tirrenia Bot game privacy

The game uses only the data needed to suggest one main choice and a plan B.

The game

Allergies, intolerances, and preferences entered as free text are processed temporarily for the current game, only after your explicit consent. The text is not included in application logs or analytics and is not retained by the server; the browser copy expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. “No dietary needs” lets you play without sharing this information.

Anonymous measurement

We may anonymously measure language, non-health answer codes, suggested product, completion, refinement, feedback, latency, cache version, and errors. We do not retain free text, IP addresses, persistent user agents, personal profiles, or dietary signals.

Any anonymous granular events are deleted within 30 days; only non-identifying aggregates remain.

Your control

Always confirm availability, ingredients, and dietary requirements with our staff before ordering. You can withdraw consent and erase the game immediately with “Start over”.
