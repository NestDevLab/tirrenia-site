Club privacy

Your Club details

This notice explains the information used to run Tirrenia Club accounts and visits.

Who is responsible

Tirrenia, Kruisweg 42, 2011 LD Haarlem, the Netherlands. KvK 96686758.

Information we use

  • Account details: name, email, phone number and marketing consent.
  • Sign-in codes sent by email.
  • Bookings: date, time, places, party size, reference, check-in and no-show status.
  • Carnet purchases, invoices and remaining visit balance.
  • Messages you send through the feedback form.

Why we use it

We use these details to manage accounts, sign-in, bookings, visits, purchases, invoices and your feedback. Marketing consent is recorded separately.

Services involved

  • Odoo stores customer records, orders and invoices and sends related emails.
  • Stripe processes card payments through Odoo. Tirrenia does not see card numbers.
  • Cloudflare provides the website and booking service.
  • Google Workspace handles email.

Sign-in cookie

A Tirrenia cookie keeps you signed in for up to 90 days, or until you sign out.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use. You can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Privacy requests

Email info@tirrenia.eu or ask us at Kruisweg 42 Haarlem. We reply within one month.

How long we keep information

Account: until you ask us to delete it, or after 2 years without activity. Bookings and check-ins: 2 years. Orders, invoices and payments: 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires. Sign-in codes stop working after 10 minutes. Feedback messages: 1 year.
