Your request,
your details.

The new Club visit and venue enquiry forms are being prepared. They are disabled until their processing details are confirmed and this notice is complete.

What the forms would ask for

Your name and contact detail; for a Club visit, preferred date, duration, number of people, optional arrival time and any note; for a venue enquiry, an optional organisation and message. Do not enter sensitive medical or financial information.

How the details would be used

Tirrenia would use them to review and answer your request. A form submission would not reserve a seat, create a purchase or agree a transfer.

Before the forms open

The final storage provider, retention periods and contact route for data requests must be checked against the deployed backend. Until then, no form on these pages can send data.
